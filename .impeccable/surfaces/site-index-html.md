# Surface brief: landing page (site/index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a player or maker who wants to find homebrew for their hardware, or an agent user who wants the data.
Action: open a real entry for their platform; copy the Prompt Pad prompt; suggest an entry.
Proof: the real collection (counts derived from data), llms.txt and collection.json that actually exist.
Constraints: static, no build step for the site, works from file://, classic scripts, no external images. Brief-pinned world: Beam-style console boot (found with REA), serif display + pixel type, acid-lime accent, fun, 2026, AI oriented. Roll skipped because the brief pins the direction.
Unresolved: live Pages URL; real OG raster is generated from the banner.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The directory is a console you boot. The page refuses the hero-plus-card-grid directory template: a black void, a giant serif "Welcome.", and a cartridge the visitor inserts. Every later section is a scene on the same machine, and the machine's screen follows the scroll.
OWN-WORLD: Void #0b0b0b ground with paper #f2f2fd text and one accent, acid lime #dbff00, used for the cartridge label, primary actions and focus. Amber and lavender appear only inside generated pixel art. Instrument Serif for display, Silkscreen for HUD, chips and screen text, Hanken Grotesk for reading. Flat surfaces, 1px #2a2a2a rules, small radii, no glows, no gradients, no glass. Every entry and category gets a deterministic pixel creature as its identity motif.
STORY: A visitor understands in one screen that this is a map of where homebrew lives, picks the machine on their desk, sees only the places that serve it, and either opens one or copies a prompt for their own assistant. They believe the list because counts and links are real.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered on black. "Welcome." in serif at the display cap (6rem desktop) in the upper third, a drawn handheld console about 320px wide below it with a dark screen and a lime-labeled cartridge hovering above its slot, and beneath it the line "Insert the cartridge to start. +30 XP." The cartridge is the primary button. Fixed HUD in the corners: wordmark left, XP, search and skip on the right.
FORM: Sticky-device scroll scenes (Beam's own structure), position 1 on the ordered list; no seed key because the roll was not run (brief-pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
