How it works

Four questions first

The skill asks before it writes a guide. Unknown details stay open.

  1. Console. Model, revision and region.
  2. Cart and writer. Brand, model, revision, programmer or uploader, cable and SD reader.
  3. ROM file. Path, format, size, where you got it and why you can use it.
  4. Computer. Operating system, version and the tools installed.

With those answers, the assistant reads the manual for your cart and writes the steps. If it cannot read the manual, it asks for the link.

The guide

The steps you follow

The guide names the exact file, the target cart and the order of steps. You do the write in your flash cart software.

IN THE GUIDE

The cart and ROM details to check, the files to back up, the steps in your software and the result to look for.

IN YOUR SOFTWARE

Plug in the cart, pick the ROM and target, write it and check the result. Your assistant asks you to confirm before anything is erased.

Test in a compatible runtime first when one exists. Then try the console.

Safety

The safety rules

  • Backups and verification come before any write.
  • The assistant asks you to confirm the cart, port and file before a format or erase.
  • Commercial ROMs and DRM bypasses are refused.
  • A write counts as done after verification and a playtest you confirm.

This page cannot see your hardware. It never says a cart was written.
